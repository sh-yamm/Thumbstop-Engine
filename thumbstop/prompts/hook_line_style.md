## Hook line (ad caption) style guide, v4

The hook line is the ad text that runs with the promo: TikTok ad text, a Reels caption, or Meta primary text, about 125 characters visible. The clip has to hook on its own. This line has one job: make a stranger **feel a sting and need to see what happens**, from one quick read in a scrolling feed.

### Real ad texts and why they work
| Ad text | Why it works |
|---|---|
| "His in-laws think he's poor. He's a billionaire." (ReelShort) | **Power reversal.** Others' contempt, then the hidden truth. The reader knows more than the characters (dramatic irony) |
| "She married a stranger on a whim, only to find out he's a trillionaire CEO!" (ReelShort) | Trope words ("stranger", "CEO") and a reveal |
| "Your ex returns…Will you tell him the kid is his?" (ReelShort) | Second person, which puts the reader inside the dilemma; it ends on a question |
| "Knowing her daughter was bullied, the Wolf Queen seeks her revenge..." (ReelShort) | Wound, then a promised payback; the "…" leaves it open |
| "…the hottest guy in school shows up at her door...naked." (ReelShort) | The ellipsis holds the punch word back until the very end |
| "My whole circle knew my husband was cheating" (Meta storytelling ad) | First-person wound and public humiliation, in one breath |
| "My dad's side piece showed up to the anniversary party" (Meta storytelling ad) | A concrete, specific situation that is instantly understood |
| "POV: your fake fiancé shows up" | POV casts the reader in the role |

Patterns that ad analysts report for short-drama ads:
- **Power reversal:** humiliation that the reader knows will flip.
- **Dangerous protection:** a protector who is also a threat.
- **A relationship under pressure.**
- **Cut at the peak:** stop before the answer.
- **A question beats a statement:** "She married the enemy, on purpose?" beats "A revenge romance."

### Write it like this
- **Open on the wound or the stakes** in the first few words: "My own sister…", "They framed her husband…", "Crawl, or…".
- **Leave it open.** End on a question, an unfinished threat ("…or he hangs"), or a reversal the reader can't wait to see land. **Never say how it ends.**
- **One vivid, concrete detail** from the clip (the letter, the knife, the locked door) beats a list of events.
- **Use the show's engine when the clip is about it.** If the clip shows contempt for someone the title says is secretly powerful, the reversal formula ("They think X. He's Y.") is the strongest line you can write.
- **Length:** 6–14 words and **at most 90 characters, counted**; most strong lines are 40–75. One or two short sentences.
- **At most one quote per line.** A back-and-forth of quoted dialogue reads like a script, not an ad.

### Never write
- **A scene description of two flat actions:** "He opens the door. She steps back." That describes the clip; it doesn't sell it.
- **The outcome:** "…and she won", "…and they fled". The cliffhanger is the product.
- **A bare quote with no framing,** unless the quote alone is a gut punch.
- **Riddles:** clever lines that a stranger can't decode in one read.

### Hard rules (code and a fact checker enforce these; a line that breaks one is dropped)
1. **Grounded.** Every fact must be backed by the evidence you cite:
   - event IDs from the promo's episodes or earlier ones;
   - episode loglines, written L plus the episode number (e.g. L007);
   - `TITLE`.

   Never use anything from an episode after the promo's last episode.
2. **True, not upgraded.** Keep who did what, to whom and with what. "Pushed" is not "beat"; one guard is not "the guards"; "planned to" is not "did".
3. **Exact quotes.** Words inside quotation marks must be the script's own words, in the same order, shortened only with "…". A single scare-quoted word ("beggar") must be a word the script uses.
4. **No character names and no place names.** Use roles a stranger understands: her sister, her husband, the judge.
5. **Clear pronouns.** The first "she" or "he" must be unambiguous. If two women appear, name one by role.
6. **No emojis, hashtags or links.**
